Account & Data Deletion

Last updated: January 2026

This page explains how to delete your account and associated data for the Cerv and Cerv Partner mobile apps, operated by Cerv, LLC ("CERVR.ai," "we," "us"). You can request deletion from inside the app or by contacting us — no purchase or login is required to read these instructions.

Delete from within the app

Open the app and go to Profile → Delete account. Confirm the request; we email you to verify it, then permanently delete your account.

Request by email

If you can't access the app, email cervappdeveloper@cervr.ai from the address on your account with the subject "Account deletion request." We may ask you to verify ownership before proceeding.

What is deleted

On completion we delete the personal data tied to your account: your name, email, phone number, and password; your properties, addresses, and access notes; your saved service lines, messages, and in-app activity. This data is removed within 30 days of a verified request.

What is retained, and for how long

We retain a limited set of records only where the law requires it or to resolve disputes and prevent fraud — for example, transaction, billing, and tax records — for the period required by applicable law (typically up to 7 years), after which they are deleted. Retained records are kept secured and are not used to re-identify you for any other purpose.
